The method

Every pair needs evidence.

This format will be visually simple and editorially strict.

01 · Identify

Find the original place.

Confirm the location, approximate date, photographer or collection, and what is actually visible in the frame.

02 · Clear rights

Permission before publication.

Publicly viewable does not mean free to reproduce. Historic creative work stays out until the rights position is clear.

03 · Re-photograph

Match the angle.

Use buildings, horizon lines, roads, coastline, trees or landmarks to reproduce the original viewpoint as closely as possible.

04 · Explain

What changed—and what didn't?

Add a short reported note: people, use, development, ecology, transport, architecture or community memory.
