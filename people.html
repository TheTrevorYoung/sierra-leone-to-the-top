Visual standard

Portraits should show people in context.

As original and rights-cleared photography grows, profiles should favor environmental portraits, action and detail over generic stock images.

What we look for

Do not stop the story at recognition.

The better question is what happened next: what was built, who benefited, what became difficult and what others can learn.
