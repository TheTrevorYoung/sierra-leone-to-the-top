Business Radar · September 2026

Five startups. Five different problems to solve.

A recent READY Salone Investor Day offers a useful snapshot of the range of businesses emerging from Sierra Leone — from tools for market traders to localized artificial intelligence.

Source-based briefing · Not an endorsement or ranking by Sierra Leone To The Top
What happened

Sierra Leone’s Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation said 11 investment-ready startups pitched at READY Salone Investor Day. Five were selected for international technology events.

MoCTI news source ↗

The most interesting part of the announcement is not simply who placed first. It is the variety of problems the finalists are trying to solve. The five businesses named by the Ministry operate across financial tools for small businesses, clean energy, urban waste, agriculture and local-language technology.

01
Small-business tools

MiBuks

The Ministry describes MiBuks as a Freetown-based digital platform for market traders and micro, small and medium-sized businesses. Its tools include trading records, expense tracking, inventory management and credit scoring intended to help businesses build financial records and access inventory financing.

MoCTI announced MiBuks as first place and selected it for COMEUP Festival 2026 in South Korea.

02
Bioenergy

Greentech Bioenergy

Greentech Bioenergy is described as developing biogas digester technology designed to turn biodegradable waste into cooking gas and organic fertilizer.

MoCTI announced Greentech Bioenergy as second place and selected it alongside MiBuks for COMEUP Festival 2026.

03
Urban climate technology

GreenBin

The Ministry says GreenBin is working on smart waste collection, community waste reporting and organic fertilizer production, combining urban-service problems with environmental technology.

GreenBin was among three startups the Ministry said were selected for Africa Tech Festival in South Africa.

04
Climate-smart agriculture

GCAD

Green Compost in Agriculture for Development (GCAD) is described as turning organic market waste and coastal seaweed into compost fertilizer, linking waste recovery with soil health, agriculture and employment.

GCAD was also announced for Africa Tech Festival.

05
Localized artificial intelligence

Geneline-X

Geneline-X is building localized language technology. The Ministry highlights Krio-to-English speech recognition and no-code chatbot tools designed for low-connectivity environments.

Geneline-X completed the five startups named for international technology events.

Why this matters

The five businesses are not proof that Sierra Leone’s startup ecosystem has solved its financing, infrastructure or scaling challenges. They are evidence of something narrower and still important: entrepreneurs are attempting to build products around problems that are deeply local — market-trader recordkeeping, organic waste, urban services, agricultural inputs and language access.

That is the kind of innovation Sierra Leone To The Top wants to follow: not technology for the sake of appearing modern, but tools whose value can be explained in terms of a real user and a real problem.

Source note: company descriptions and competition results above are summarized from the Sierra Leone Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation’s August 3, 2026 announcement and related public material. Sierra Leone To The Top has not independently audited company performance, financing, product claims or future event attendance.
