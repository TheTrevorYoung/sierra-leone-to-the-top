Trust & accountability

Editorial Standards

Optimism is stronger when people can trust the facts underneath it.

Our standard

Publish constructively. Verify carefully. Correct openly.

VerificationArchiveCorrectionsMedia rightsSponsorshipAI-assisted toolsNonpartisanship

Sierra Leone To The Top exists to promote progress without becoming propaganda. That requires a clear line between what we know, what someone claims, what we infer, and what still needs verification.

01

Verification & sourcing

Factual claims should be supported by direct evidence, credible public records, named sources, firsthand reporting or authoritative organizations. Where a source is useful to readers, we link to it.

We do not manufacture partnerships, achievements, statistics, biographies or quotes. If something cannot yet be verified, we either say so or leave it out.

02

Archive material

Older Sierra Leone To The Top material is valuable historical evidence, but it is not automatically current reporting. Archive material is labeled with its date and context. We do not add new biographies or claims to an old title simply because a name appears in the archive.

03

Corrections

When a material factual error is confirmed, we correct the public content rather than silently protecting the mistake. Significant corrections should be noted on the affected story where practical.

Until the official editorial email is connected, correction leads can be sent through a connected Sierra Leone To The Top social channel.

04

Photographs, video & contributed media

We use media we created, control, licensed, received with permission, or are otherwise legally entitled to publish. Third-party media should carry appropriate credit or source information. Contributors must have the right to provide material they submit.

Editorial context should not falsely imply that a person, business or organization endorses Sierra Leone To The Top.

05

Sponsorship & commercial relationships

A business may support Sierra Leone To The Top, but payment cannot secretly purchase favorable editorial treatment. Sponsored or paid content must be clearly labeled. Editorial profiles should be selected because the story is worthwhile, not because someone paid to appear.

06

AI-assisted tools

Technology, including AI-assisted tools, may support research organization, transcription, translation, editing, design, coding or production. The same verification standard applies regardless of which tools help produce the work.

AI-generated text, images or summaries should not be used to fabricate documentary evidence, quotations, people, events or partnerships. Material presented as a real photograph or real-world record must be grounded in real source material.

07

Independent & nonpartisan

Sierra Leone To The Top is not the communications arm of a political party, government or opposition movement. Public policy and institutions may be covered when relevant to national progress, but the platform does not exist to campaign for candidates or parties.

Constructive criticism is compatible with national pride. So is acknowledging genuine progress.

The promise

National pride without lowering the standard of proof.
