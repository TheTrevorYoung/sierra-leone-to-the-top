The 2020 Conversations.

Before the current relaunch, Sierra Leone To The Top was already using live video to create a space for conversation. The existing YouTube archive preserves a run of IG Live titles from May and June 2020.

Historical archive · Titles preserved from the existing channel
Archive standard

This page records what the channel itself preserves. We do not add biographies, claims or context about guests until those details are independently reviewed.

An early version of the idea

The format was simple: conversations under the Sierra Leone To The Top name, published during a concentrated stretch in 2020. That history matters because the 2026 platform is not starting from a blank page. It is building on an earlier instinct to connect people and give Sierra Leonean voices a platform.

Archive first. Add context only when we can verify it.

Beyond the live series

The channel also preserves a later video titled Memuna Barnes Jackson “SURVIVED: The Journey”. It remains part of the historical collection and can be developed into a fuller archive entry after the original episode context is reviewed.

What changes in the relaunch

The archive stays visible, but new Sierra Leone To The Top video will be unmistakably original: our interviews, field footage, narration, reporting, commentary and editing. Historical material will be clearly labeled as archive material rather than presented as new reporting.
