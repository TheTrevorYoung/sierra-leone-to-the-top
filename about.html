About the platform

…land that we love.

Sierra Leone To The Top exists to help move Sierra Leone forward.

Foundation v1.0

Our mission

To promote, connect and support people, ideas, initiatives and opportunities that contribute to the advancement of Sierra Leone.

Our vision

A confident, connected, innovative and prosperous Sierra Leone in which its people have the opportunity and capacity to reach their full potential.

Our purpose

Sierra Leone To The Top is a platform for promoting the people, ideas, achievements, opportunities, institutions, businesses, communities and initiatives that can contribute to a stronger and more prosperous Sierra Leone.

Progress should be promoted, connected and multiplied.

Our principles

Sierra Leone first

We ask whether the work contributes positively to Sierra Leone or Sierra Leoneans.

Independent and nonpartisan

We are not an arm of a political party, government, opposition movement or politician. Good ideas can come from anywhere and problems should be examined on evidence.

Constructive

Challenges should be discussed honestly, but whenever possible criticism should point toward solutions.

Inclusive

The platform belongs to a vision of Sierra Leone that includes every region, community, generation and Sierra Leonean around the world.

Credible

Claims should be accurate, achievements should not be exaggerated and relationships should never be implied where none exist.

Action-oriented

The platform should ultimately be judged by what it helps make possible, not simply by how much content it publishes.

What we are not

We are not a political party, government agency, propaganda platform or a brand centered on one individual. We are building a platform for Sierra Leonean progress.

A platform with history

Relaunched, not invented yesterday.

The current platform is broader and more structured, but the Sierra Leone To The Top name and media activity have a documented history.

2017

Video blog + early commercial use

Preserved Facebook links document the Sierra Leone To The Top Video Blog publishing in July 2017. A later U.S. trademark application separately recorded September 28, 2017 as the claimed first-use date for apparel.

Explore the 2017 archive →

2020

Video and conversation

The Sierra Leone To The Top YouTube archive preserves a run of IG Live conversation titles from May and June 2020. A U.S. trademark application for the name was filed that same year.

2026

A broader relaunch

The brand is being rebuilt as an independent national-progress platform spanning people, business, culture, places, communities, diaspora, youth and ideas.

Historical note: the 2017 and 2020 brand-history dates above are based on the public trademark filing record and the existing Sierra Leone To The Top YouTube archive. This timeline describes brand history; it does not imply that the prior trademark application is currently active.
