Our video standard

Original by design.

Sierra Leone To The Top video will be built around original interviews, field footage, narration, reporting, commentary and editing. When archival or contributed material is used, its role and source should be clear.

The goal is not simply to show Sierra Leone. It is to help people understand it.

Every strong video should be able to become more: a website feature, short clips, photographs, quotes and useful social content — all pointing back to the larger story.
