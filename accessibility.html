Site commitment

Accessibility

Sierra Leone To The Top should be usable by as many people as practical, across devices, connection speeds and ways of navigating the web.

Our target

We aim toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA practices as the site develops. This is a target and design standard — not a claim that every page has completed a formal third-party accessibility audit.

What is built into the current site

  • Semantic page structure and a consistent main-content landmark.
  • A “Skip to content” link for keyboard users.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and native links/buttons.
  • Responsive layouts designed to reflow on phones and smaller screens.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful photographs and visual content.
  • Color choices designed for readable contrast rather than decoration alone.
  • No essential interaction that depends only on hover.

Low-bandwidth usability matters too

Accessibility in Sierra Leone also includes practical network conditions. The site is being built as a lightweight static website without a heavy application framework, and we will continue to optimize images and avoid unnecessary scripts.

Video and social content

As new original video is produced, the goal is to add useful titles, descriptions and captions or transcripts where practical. Archive material may not always meet the same production standard, and should be clearly identified as archive content.

Report a problem

If something prevents you from reading, navigating or understanding the site, we want to hear about it. Please identify the page, the problem and — if helpful — the device or assistive technology involved.

Contact us about accessibility

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is not a one-time checklist. New content, forms, maps, video players and third-party integrations will need to be reviewed as they are introduced.
