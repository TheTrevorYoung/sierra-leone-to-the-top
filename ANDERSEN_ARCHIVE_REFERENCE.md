# Peter C. Andersen / Sierra Leone Web — SLTT Archive Reference

## Purpose
This is an editorial and media-reference file for Sierra Leone To The Top. It records useful structures and source trails from Peter C. Andersen's Sierra Leone Web that can strengthen SLTT without copying another site's identity or republishing material without rights clearance.

## What SLTT is adopting

### 1. A living archive, not a dead storage page
Archive material should lead back into current reporting. A historic photograph, postcard, map, document, book, language item or news record should become a starting point for a contemporary story.

### 2. Then & Now
Use documented historical material to identify places and subjects worth revisiting today. The contemporary side should increasingly use SLTT-owned or commissioned photography/video.

Candidate subjects include:
- Freetown streets, waterfronts and markets
- Lumley and Aberdeen
- Bunce Island
- historic schools and institutions
- former railway sites and transport corridors
- neighborhoods visible in historic postcards
- coastal and landscape views

The goal is continuity, not forced same-angle imitation.

### 3. From the Archive
Create short, highly visual archive-led stories around one strong source item: a photograph, map, document, object, language item or historic report. Label the period, source, rights status and what is known or uncertain.

### 4. Go Deeper
Substantive SLTT stories should increasingly end with a compact research trail when useful:
- original documents
- maps
- archive material
- books and bibliography
- data
- interviews
- relevant external sources

The story remains readable for a general audience; deeper evidence is available for readers who want it.

### 5. Searchable country knowledge
Andersen's work demonstrates the value of organizing Sierra Leone material by subject rather than leaving it scattered. SLTT should preserve strong navigation and search across people, place, culture, enterprise, diaspora, archive and ideas.

### 6. Language as culture
Krio, Mende, Temne, Susu, Sherbro and other language resources can inspire reporting about identity, humor, memory, place names, proverbs and everyday expression. Language should be treated as living culture, not merely reference material.

### 7. Provenance is part of the story
For archive-led work, preserve a visible chain of provenance wherever possible:
- creator or photographer
- collection or publisher
- approximate or known date
- original caption/context
- rights/reuse status
- URL or bibliographic source
- SLTT verification notes

Do not strip attribution when material moves into an editorial workflow.

## Rights rule
Do not download or republish Sierra Leone Web photography, scans or other copyrighted archive material merely because it is publicly viewable. Link to it, cite it and use it as research/creative reference unless reuse permission or a compatible license has been confirmed. Track source and rights status for every asset selected for possible publication.

## Commissioning rule
Public archive material can serve as a creative brief for an SLTT photographer or videographer. The commissioned assignment should capture today's Sierra Leone from an informed contemporary perspective and produce SLTT-owned original media wherever practical.
