What makes a strong lead

  • A clear connection to Sierra Leone or Sierra Leoneans.
  • Something concrete that can be verified.
  • A useful, interesting or constructive story for the public.
  • Enough information for us to identify and contact the person or organization.

Submission channel

Story nominations will open with the public launch. In the meantime, follow Sierra Leone To The Top on our connected channels and begin thinking about the people, businesses, places and ideas that deserve wider attention.

Editorial independence

A nomination is a lead, not a promise of coverage. Sierra Leone To The Top will verify material before publication and retains editorial independence over what it features.
