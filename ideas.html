Constructive by design

This is where difficult subjects can be examined without turning the platform into either a complaint machine or a collection of unrealistic promises.

Constructive does not mean avoiding problems. It means doing the work required to move beyond them.

The operating question

Every substantial Ideas & Solutions feature should start by making the problem specific, establishing what evidence exists, understanding what is already being tried, confronting real-world constraints and identifying a useful next action.
