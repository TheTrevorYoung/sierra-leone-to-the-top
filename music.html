A short guide to Sierra Leonean music.

From palm-wine guitar and bubu to Krio fusion, rap and Afrobeats.

Palm-wine · Maringa

The guitar tradition.

Palm-wine music—often called maringa in Sierra Leone—grew from coastal guitar traditions and became a defining popular sound through artists including Ebenezer Calendar and S. E. Rogie.

HistorySierra Leone culture reference
Bubu

Procession, rhythm, reinvention.

Bubu is a Temne musical tradition built around flutes, pipes and percussion. Janka Nabay helped bring a modernized version to wider audiences.

Krio fusion · Now

Tradition meets the present.

Contemporary artists blend Krio, rap, R&B, Afrobeats and older Sierra Leonean sounds. Drizilik describes his approach as Krio fusion.
