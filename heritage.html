Archive & Heritage

The past is not background. It is part of the story.

We are building a visual, source-linked record that connects Sierra Leone's places, people, names, maps and memory to the country as it exists now.

Explore Then & NowOpen the Research Shelf
Preserve it. Verify it. Bring it back to life.

Sierra Leone has a deep digital and physical record, but much of it is scattered across archives, books, collections and personal memory. SLTT will not simply reproduce old material. We will use sources to create new reporting, new photography and new ways to see the country.

One important external reference is The Sierra Leone Web ↗, founded by Peter C. Andersen in 1996. Its long-running archive includes photographs, news archives, maps, documents, books, laws, cultural material, village-name histories and other resources. SLTT is independent from that project; links here are source references, not an implied partnership.

A source, not a shortcut.

When an external archive points us toward a photograph, map, document or story, we still check provenance, dates, rights and context. We link to the original source whenever possible. Creative work is not republished just because it is publicly viewable.
