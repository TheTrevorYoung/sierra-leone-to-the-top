Diaspora

Sierra Leone is larger than its borders.

Global Sierra Leone

The diaspora is not simply a source of remittances. It is a network of knowledge, professional experience, relationships, capital, creativity and people who remain part of Sierra Leone’s story wherever they live.

Connection should make participation easier, not more ceremonial.

Five practical pathways

ReturnStories of moving back, visiting with purpose, or building across two homes.
MentorTransfer skills and experience to people who can use them.
BuildCreate businesses, projects, networks and professional bridges.
InvestUnderstand opportunities with evidence rather than hype.
ConnectOpen doors to markets, institutions, expertise and relationships.
